productivitycal — privacy policy

last updated: 2026-08-28

productivitycal ("the app") is a calendar and wallpaper-automation app. this policy explains what data the app handles.

sign-in

the app lets you sign in with Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In, solely to identify you across app launches. we receive a basic identifier and, if you grant it, your name and email address from the provider. this information is used only to keep you signed in on your device and is not sent to any server we operate — the app has no backend of its own.

data stored on your device

your daily mood entries, calendar history, and app preferences (theme, selected iPhone model, wallpaper style) are stored locally on your device only. they are never uploaded to any server and are not shared with third parties. deleting the app, or using "delete account" inside the app, permanently removes this data from your device.

notifications

if you allow it, the app schedules local reminder notifications on your device. these are generated and delivered entirely on-device; no notification content is sent to or from a server.

third-party services

the app uses Sign in with Apple (Apple Inc.) and Google Sign-In (Google LLC) solely for authentication. each provider's own privacy policy governs the data they process as part of that sign-in.

data deletion

you can remove all app data at any time from within the app: account menu → delete account. since all data is stored locally, this immediately and completely erases it from your device.
