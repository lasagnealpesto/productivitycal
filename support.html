common questions

the app asks for premium / a purchase.
it shouldn't — every feature in productivitycal is free. if you see anything referencing a purchase, please email us with a screenshot.

i deleted the app / changed phone and lost my calendar history.
your data (mood entries, calendar, preferences) is stored only on your device for privacy — it isn't backed up to a server, so reinstalling or switching devices starts fresh.

the automatic wallpaper isn't updating.
this relies on an iOS Shortcuts personal automation you set up once (open the app's "set" tab → "set wallpaper" → follow the steps). if the automation stopped firing, check Shortcuts → Automation and make sure it's still enabled and "ask before running" is off.

see also our privacy policy.
